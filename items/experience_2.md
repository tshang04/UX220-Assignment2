# Swimming Lessons Experience

Another experience I had in the past was taking swimming lessons, also from a young age until my mid teens. I enjoyed this more than piano because I like the feeling of swimming, and I view it as a necessary life skill that can come in handy at crucial moments. I also liked the group format of the swimming lessons; I took my lessons at the local community centre with a small group of students around my age, so I could have fun during classes and get to know everyone. I also enjoyed it because I felt like I was quite good at swimming, so I was proud of my accomplishment. 

**Favourite memory:** My favourite memory is going down the water slide at the end of every lesson, because it felt like a reward for my hard work.

**Challenge:** Something I didn’t enjoy was jumping off the diving board, since jumping off the higher one felt scary but I would feel pressured to do it if other students were jumping.

**Overall rating:** B
