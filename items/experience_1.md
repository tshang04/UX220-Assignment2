# Piano Lessons Experience

I used to take piano lessons from the time I was 9 until I turned 14. Although I played instruments such as the recorder and violin at school, I spent more time practicing the piano and got more familiar with it because of the individualized lessons, and it felt good to learn a new skill and feel myself improving.

**Favourite memory:** My favourite memory is playing for my family, because they would always be so excited and proud to hear a new song I learned.

**Challenge:** However, something I didn’t like was that sometimes I would have to practice or attend a lesson even when I didn’t feel like it, although I guess this taught me discipline. 

**Overall rating:** C
