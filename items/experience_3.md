# Caring for Stray Animals Experience

A third experience that shaped who I am was my work taking care of rescue dogs and cats. My family loves feeding and taking care of the local strays whenever we go back to China to visit, and we also adopted quite a few of them and brought them back home. This took place around my late high school years and early university years. It was hard work because I was expected to contribute to taking care of each stray, including walking them, feeding them, and taking them to the vet when they were ill. But it was good because I felt like I was helping out a creature in need, and because I enjoy playing with animals.

**Favourite memory:** My favourite memory was when we first found our first stray dog. She was wandering around the streets and clearly hungry, so we fed her. After we left, she continued following us to show her gratitude and happiness and she started to wag her tail.

**Challenge:** Something I didn’t enjoy was the fact that taking care of so many animals left me with little personal space or time, and I would often have to view distressing scenes such as a dog or cat suffering from an illness.

**Overall rating:** A
