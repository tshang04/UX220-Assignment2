# School Play Experience

I acted in a school play in high school. My part wasn’t the biggest, but it still involved wearing a costume and memorizing lines and positioning throughout the play. We also rehearsed for arond 2 months, so it took some consistent effort. I found the experience quite fun because I felt a sense of connection with the other students working on the play, and I enjoyed watching other students play their parts. 

**Favourite memory:** A favorite memory was the night we performed the play, when the other students and I were waiting backstage for the show to start and we all felt a nervous excitement. It was a thrilling moment and it made me feel like our efforts paid off. 

**Challenge:** Something I didn’t enjoy was my nerves each time I had to go on stage for my part - I would be worried about messing up, so in the minutes before my part came up I would feel my heart beating fast and become anxious.

**Overall rating:** B
