# Church Volunteering Experience

I also did volunteer work at a church during high school. The work included tidying up the church, sorting donated goods such as items for food banks, handing out pamphlets at the door, and other miscellaneous tasks. I enjoyed getting to know the people who attended the church and I felt that the work didn’t take up too much time, since it was only once a week. 

**Favourite memory:** My favourite memory was hearing the choir practice and sing during worship services. Although this was unrelated to my work, the music was beautiful and I would enjoy listening to it while I worked or sat in on a service. 

**Challenge:** One thing I didn’t enjoy was the mundanity of the work. I generally found the tasks boring and repetitive, so that I would feel restless halfway through.

**Overall rating:** C
